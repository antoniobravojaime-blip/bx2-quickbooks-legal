BX2 QuickBooks Connector

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BX2 Global LLC collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information through the BX2 QuickBooks Connector.

This policy applies only to the Connector and its connection with QuickBooks Online.

1. Information We Collect

QuickBooks Information

The Connector may receive authorized company identity, chart-of-accounts, general-ledger, account-balance, customer, vendor, invoice, bill, purchase, check, credit, journal-entry, date, amount, reference, classification, and financial-report information.

Authorization Information

The Connector may store OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, authorization dates, refresh dates, QuickBooks environment information, and connection status. BX2 does not collect or store QuickBooks passwords through the Connector.

Technical Information

BX2 may collect request dates, Connector status, error messages, service-health information, application logs, security events, authentication events, and browser-origin information.

2. How We Use Information

BX2 may use information to maintain the QuickBooks connection, retrieve authorized data, generate reports, normalize and classify transactions, compare approved sources, detect missing information, provide reconciliation and control reporting, display source identity, refresh tokens, provide support, improve the Connector, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Authorization

BX2 processes QuickBooks information based on authorization from the user connecting the company, performance of requested services, legitimate operational and security interests, and applicable legal obligations.

4. Information Sharing

BX2 does not sell information obtained through the Connector.

Information may be shared with service providers, Intuit, professional advisers, legal authorities, or parties involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, when reasonably necessary and subject to applicable protections.

5. Data Storage

Authorization information may be stored on secure hosted infrastructure. QuickBooks information may be processed or stored as reasonably necessary to provide reporting, validation, reconciliation, audit, support, and operational functions.

6. Data Retention

BX2 retains information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Connector, maintain security, support audit and financial-control requirements, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or comply with legal obligations.

7. Disconnection and Deletion

Authorized users may disconnect the QuickBooks company through Connector controls or revoke access through Intuit.

Access, correction, or deletion requests may be sent to admin@bxtwo.com. BX2 may verify the requester's identity and authority before processing a request.

8. Security

BX2 uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including HTTPS, restricted access, protected token storage, environment-based credential management, authentication validation, file permissions, source-code and secret separation, security logging, and deployment review.

No electronic transmission or storage system is completely secure.

9. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for protecting their Intuit and QuickBooks accounts, authorizing appropriate personnel, revoking access when no longer needed, and informing BX2 of suspected unauthorized use.

10. International Processing

BX2 and its service providers may process information in the United States or other locations where they operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

11. Children's Privacy

The Connector is intended for businesses and authorized adult business users. BX2 does not knowingly use it to collect personal information from children under 13.

12. Third-Party Services

The Connector interacts with Intuit and QuickBooks Online. Intuit's independent collection and use of information are governed by Intuit's own terms and privacy practices.

13. Advertising and Sale

BX2 does not sell Connector information and does not use QuickBooks information for third-party behavioral advertising.

14. Policy Changes

BX2 may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Connector, law, security practices, or operations. Updated versions will be posted with a revised effective date.
